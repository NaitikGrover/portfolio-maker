# Test Scenario 3: Senior UI/UX & Product Designer (Minimalist Editorial)

This scenario tests an aesthetic request for Swiss Minimalist / Editorial typography with deep-dive case studies and dual-mode toggling.

---

## 👤 User Profile & Prompt

**User Prompt:**
> "I want a clean, minimalist portfolio like Dieter Rams or Apple design. I'm Elena Chen, a Senior Product Designer focused on design systems and fintech interfaces. I want light and dark mode, elegant typography, and case study highlights."

---

## 🤖 AI Execution Steps

1. **Intake Processing**:
   * AI recognizes role: Senior Product Designer.
   * Preferred aesthetic: Swiss Minimalist / Editorial with high typography focus (`Cormorant Garamond` serif headings paired with clean `Inter` sans body).
   * Mode: Dual-mode (System default + toggle switch).

2. **Case Study Structuring**:
   * AI gathers or structures 2-3 flagship case studies with quantifiable impact:
     * *Aura: Scalable Multi-Brand Design System* (Unified 4 product suites, reduced handoff friction by 55%).
     * *Vantage: Wealth Dashboard for Retail Investors* (+42% recurring deposit growth, 4.9★ rating).
   * Formats a 3-pillar Design Philosophy section: *Clarity, Systems, Delight*.

3. **Generation**:
   * Generates `templates/creative-minimalist/index.html`.
   * High-contrast whitespace, subtle hairline dividers, zero visual clutter.
   * Smooth persistent theme toggle between stone white (`#fafaf9`) and graphite dark (`#121212`).
